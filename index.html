About Us

Mulugeta Haile Digital Horticulture

Digital Horticulture is a forward-thinking horticultural consulting initiative dedicated to transforming agriculture and horticulture through digital technology, data-driven decision-making, and innovative management solutions.

With a strong background in Horticulture and practical experience in the floriculture industry, we understand the real-world challenges faced by growers, farms, and horticultural businesses.

Our Mission

To provide practical and innovative consulting solutions that connect horticultural expertise, digital technology, data, and modern management to create measurable value for horticultural businesses.

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in digital horticultural consulting, helping transform African horticulture into a more productive, innovative, data-driven, and sustainable industry.

What We Believe

We believe that the future of horticulture lies in combining knowledge, technology, data, and people. By embracing digital transformation, horticultural businesses can make better decisions, reduce losses, improve efficiency, and build sustainable growth.

Based in Addis Ababa and Holeta, we work with the needs of Ethiopia's horticultural sector in mind.
